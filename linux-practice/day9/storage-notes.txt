Day 9 - Linux Disk and Storage Basics

df -h
Shows filesystem disk usage in human-readable format.

du -sh .
Shows the total size of the current directory.

lsblk
Lists block devices such as disks and swap.

Windows C: is mounted in WSL as /mnt/c.
